# Mistakes & Weak Areas

This folder contains the **questions, concepts, coding problems, and interview topics that I failed to answer or could not explain clearly**.

This is one of the most important sections of my preparation system.

## 🎯 Purpose

The goal is not to record every mistake.

The goal is to identify recurring weaknesses and eliminate them.

## ❌ What Should Be Added?

Add something here when:

- I cannot answer an interview question
- I give an incorrect answer
- I know the concept but cannot explain it
- I forget an important detail
- I cannot provide a practical example
- I struggle with a coding problem
- I receive negative feedback during a mock interview
- I repeatedly make the same mistake

## 📂 Categories

## 📝 Mistake Format

Each mistake should contain:

### Question / Problem

What was asked?

### My Answer

What did I answer?

### Correct Understanding

What should I have answered?

### Why I Failed

Why couldn't I answer correctly?

### Action

What will I do to fix the weakness?

### Status

- 🔴 Not fixed
- 🟡 Practicing
- 🟢 Fixed
